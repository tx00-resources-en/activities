# Pair programming event

The pair programming event will be **after the 17th of September**. This activity is designed to enhance your programming skills and foster collaboration within our class.

During the event, you will be randomly assigned a pair member. One of you will take on the role of the "driver," responsible for writing code, while the other will be the "navigator," tasked with reviewing the code and providing guidance. This dynamic duo approach will help you learn from one another and improve your problem-solving abilities.

I am confident that you will manage this exercise effectively and find it both enjoyable and educational. Pair programming is an excellent way to practice coding in a team setting, a skill that is highly valued in the software development industry.
