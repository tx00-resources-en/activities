### Feedback 5%

- `AVOP2 Questionnaire`, is administered at the Metropolia level and is not exclusive to any specific course. It is distributed to all second-year students at Metropolia to gather feedback on their educational experience at the institution.
- `Feedback` about `web courses`: The purpose of this feedback is twofold: firstly, to provide insights into your experiences, including the challenges you encountered and your strategies for overcoming them during the course, and secondly, to offer recommendations for future students undertaking the same course.
